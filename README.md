# My Favorite Movie

## The Incredible Hulk (2008)

**The Incredible Hulk** is my favorite superhero movie.
It follows Bruce Banner, a scientist who transforms
into the Hulk when he becomes angry.

### Why I Like It
- The action scenes are exciting.
- Hulk is strong and protects people.
- Bruce tries to control his power.

### My Favorite Character
My favorite character is **Bruce Banner / Hulk**
because he is *strong and determined*.

### What I Learned
The movie taught me that having great power
also means being responsible for how we use it.
